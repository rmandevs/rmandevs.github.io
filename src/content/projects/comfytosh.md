Comfytosh is a colour theme in the tradition of Catppuccin, Dracula and Nord: a named palette, two flavors and ready-made ports for editors, terminals and command line tools.

**Screen** is the glass of the monitor: gunmetal greys with phosphor greens. **Case** is the beige plastic around it. Warm neutrals, low-saturation accents and nothing pure black or pure white, so the screen rests the eye and the colour does the work.

- 21 ports, from Neovim, VS Code and Zed to Ghostty, tmux and lazygit
- One `palette.json` that every port is mapped from
- Every text colour is checked for contrast on its flavor's ground

The site has every port as a download, a live preview and the full palette.
